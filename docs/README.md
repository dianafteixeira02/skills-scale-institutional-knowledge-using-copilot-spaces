# OctoAcme Project Management Processes Summary and Links

This README provides a concise overview of OctoAcme's project management processes and links to the detailed process documentation.

## Project Management Process Overview

OctoAcme uses a consistent, iterative lifecycle across projects:

- **Initiation:** Confirm the business need and measurable outcome, identify stakeholders and champions, define success criteria and high-level milestones, assess initial risks and resources, and decide whether work should move into planning.
- **Planning:** Turn an approved initiative into an actionable plan by defining scope and a prioritized backlog, estimating work, establishing the Definition of Done, identifying dependencies and risks, and agreeing on a release timeline.
- **Execution and tracking:** Deliver small, testable increments through a shared project board and pull-request workflow. Teams use standups, delivery syncs, demos, automated quality checks, metrics, and regular risk reviews to manage progress and escalate blockers.
- **Release and deployment:** Verify acceptance criteria, quality gates, release notes, rollback plans, and smoke tests before deploying to staging and production, followed by post-deployment checks and stakeholder communication.
- **Retrospective and continuous improvement:** After sprints, releases, incidents, or milestones, teams capture what went well and what can improve, assign owners and due dates to action items, and review their impact.

Clear role ownership, regular communication, and a single source of truth for status help keep delivery aligned while supporting psychological safety and data-informed decisions.
